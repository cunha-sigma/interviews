# Structured Feedback Criteria for Senior MLOps / ML Platform Engineer

Transcribed as-is from `Interview Criteria and Questions.pdf`.

## English level

Fluency in using appropriate vocabulary and grammar, following business ethics.

- Rating: 1 to 5 bars
  - 1 BAR = Basic level
  - 2 BARS = Pre-Intermediate
  - 3 BARS = Intermediate
  - 4 BARS = Upper-Intermediate
  - 5 BARS = Advanced
- Feedback: general feedback about English level

## Interpersonal skills

Group of skills that can be checked during the interview: Receptiveness to Feedback, Collaboration, Attitude to work, Communication. You need to give an overall grade for the group with additional comments for each checked skill.

- Rating: 1 to 5 bars
  - 1 BAR = None
  - 2 BARS = Basic: Demonstrated rarely / No expertise sufficient to spread and develop skill in other people
  - 3 BARS = Good: Demonstrated sometimes, may require prompting / No or pointwise help to others in skill development
  - 4 BARS = Strong: Consistently demonstrated / Able to encourage and help others with skill strengthening and development
  - 5 BARS = Expert: Consistently demonstrated in various situations / Consistently provides others with guidelines to strengthen and develop skill
- Feedback: general feedback about the topic, with comments per checked skill (Receptiveness to Feedback, Collaboration, Attitude to work, Communication)

## Analytical skills

Group of skills that can be checked during the interview: Ability to collect and analyze information, Problem-solving, Decision making. You need to give an overall grade for the group with additional comments for each checked skill.

- Rating: 1 to 5 bars
  - 1 BAR = None
  - 2 BARS = Basic: Has knowledge and skills, but didn't implement it on practice on the regular basis or not at all
  - 3 BARS = Good: Demonstrated sometimes, may require prompting / No or pointwise help to others in skill development
  - 4 BARS = Strong: Is capable of self-reflection, can independently analyze the situation; based on analyzed information, creates a roadmap in order to work on mistakes
  - 5 BARS = Expert: Helps colleagues to develop, acts as a supervisor/mentor. Source of expertise
- Feedback: general feedback about the topic, with comments per checked skill (Ability to collect and analyze information, Problem-solving, Decision making)

Specify the candidate's seniority level based on the interview results (e.g. Intern / Junior / Middle / Senior / Principal). Briefly justify your assessment.
