## Candidate's Level

- Rating: 4 / 5 bars (Senior)

---

## Recommendation

Borderline. Where his technical answers could be clearly understood, they were consistently strong and showed genuine depth across ML fundamentals, LLM engineering trade-offs, and applied problem-solving. But communication was a real, significant obstacle — hard to follow, unfocused, and slow to improve even when prompted directly — and it limited how much could be verified overall, including entire topic areas that weren't reached in the time available. A good next step would be a follow-up conversation structured around short, direct questions specifically to get cleaner signal on communication in a working context, before making a final call.
