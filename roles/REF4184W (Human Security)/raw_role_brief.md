# Data Scientist (Analytics/Insights track, non-ML) / Product Data Scientist / Analytics Engineer

We're looking for a Data Scientist to join a newly forming team tackling long-standing data and analytics challenges across client's Enterprise product line. This is not a modeling or machine-learning role - instead, it calls for a general-purpose, highly independent problem-solver with strong engineering instincts: someone who can look at a complex, live data system, understand why it's built the way it is, and figure out how to make it simpler and more efficient.

The first project involves analyzing the existing set of detection logic - complex rule-based expressions built on multiple data signals - that the team currently maintains. While the product itself is cybersecurity-focused, this specific project is not about bot traffic or threat analysis; it's about understanding data flows, identifying redundancy and complexity, and finding ways to simplify and reduce the number of detections without sacrificing effectiveness. You'll need to trace how data is gathered and processed, question why it's structured a certain way, and assess the downstream impact of any changes you propose.

Beyond the initial project, you'll be expected to operate with a high degree of autonomy in a fast-moving environment, working closely with engineers, data scientists, and security researchers to gather context, validate assumptions, and move work forward without waiting on hand-offs.

## Responsibilities

- Analyze existing detection logic (rule-based expressions built on multiple signals) to understand current structure, complexity, and redundancy
- Investigate underlying data flows: how signals are gathered, processed, and used, and why they were designed that way
- Identify opportunities to simplify and consolidate detections while preserving their effectiveness
- Form hypotheses about inefficiencies or redundancies and validate them through data-driven analysis
- Assess and communicate the potential impact of proposed changes before they're implemented
- Translate technical findings into clear, actionable recommendations for both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Collaborate closely with engineers, data scientists, and security researchers to gather context and unblock your own work
- Adapt quickly to shifting priorities in a high-tempo, short-cycle environment

## Requirements

- High seniority and a strong track record of working independently
- Proficient in Python and SQL, with experience working across large, complex datasets
- Demonstrated ability to independently scope ambiguous problems, formulate hypotheses, and drive analysis to data-backed conclusions with minimal guidance
- Strong engineering understanding — able to read, navigate, and reason about complex logic/code, not just query data
- Strong ability to translate analytical findings into clear, actionable recommendations for both technical and non-technical audiences
- Adaptable and effective in a fast-paced, high-tempo environment with shifting priorities and short delivery cycles
- Collaborative mindset, with the ability to work closely with engineers, data scientists, and security researchers to gather context and resolve blockers independently
- Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
- Familiarity with JavaScript
- Familiarity with Snowflake is a nice to have
