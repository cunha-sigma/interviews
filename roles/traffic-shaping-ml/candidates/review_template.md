# Candidate Review Template

This is a template that need to be filled for each candidate. After finsihing the interview for each candidate, I need this template to be filled for the candidate.

## 1. Overall rating

- Rating: 1 to 5 stars
- Feedback: general feedback for the candidate, considering the whole interview

## 2. English level
Fluency in using appropriate vocabulary and grammar, following business ethics.

- Rating: 1 to 5 stars
- Feedback: general feedback about english level

## 3. Interpersonal skills
Group of skills that can be checked during the interview: Receptiveness to Feedback, Collaboration, Attitude to work, Communication. You need to give an overall grade for the group with additional comments for each checked skill.

- Rating: 1 to 5 stars
- Feedback: general feedback about the topic

## 4. Analytical Skills
Group of skills that can be checked during the interview: Ability to collect and analyze information, Problem-solving, Decision making. You need to give an overall grade for the group with additional comments for each checked skill.

- Rating: 1 to 5 stars
- Feedback: general feedback about the topic

## 5. Candidate's Level
Specify the candidate’s seniority level based on the interview results (e.g. Intern / Junior / Middle / Senior / Principal).
Briefly justify your assessment.

- Rating: 1 to 5 stars
- Feedback: general feedback about the topic
